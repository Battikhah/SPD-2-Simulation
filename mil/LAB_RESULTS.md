# Lab-data experiment: per-window results (unweighted)

Generated by `mil/lab_report.py`. **One fixed split, no cross-validation, one seed:** test = healthy folders 8 and 24 (the two healthy trees every earlier model flagged; both share a recorder with infested trees) and infested folders 5, 11, 19+23, 28. Training = the other 15 trees (healthy folders 7, 9, 25), plus, for the *lab* runs, 1,450 lab pseudo-clips (483 clean / 967 infested; each two 10 s lab clips joined into 20 s), never evaluated. All runs: skip-window training, K = 12, 115,200 training bags, gated attention + linear classifier. Each window of 12 recordings counts once; threshold 0.5; 95% tree-cluster bootstrap intervals (only 2 healthy test trees, so intervals are wide). Development data only; the sealed groups were never loaded.

No pretrained MFCC encoder exists for this input (pretrained audio encoders take log-mel or waveform), so the MFCC model is a custom CNN only.
